/Compiled: April 27, 2026/

Setting

The Six Systems (The Roman Federation)

A cluster of six star systems with Byzantine cultural influences.
Intentionally interconnected --- the Fungusians hypothesize this was
part of a master plan by an ancient predecessor species that created the
Fungusians, Pantherans, Federote (bird-like spaceship builders), and
others to do their bidding. The federation's official name is suppressed
in favor of terms that suit various political factions.

New Byzantium

The breadbasket of the six systems, with the best climate for growing
human food. Cannot use this as a stranglehold on the other systems ---
scale and the need to hold the star economy together prevent it. New
Byzantium also has a lucrative seafood industry supplying off-worlders
with delicacies. The planet has two population centers:

New Byzantium City: The primary setting of the novel. A war-damaged city
roughly eighty years after the conflict --- long enough that no one
alive remembers it firsthand, but recent enough that physical and
economic damage remains visible. The aristocracy fled to New
Constantinople after the war. Those left behind eventually stabilized
basic services. Taxes are low to encourage people to stay. Governed by
power brokers rather than traditional authority. Violent and dangerous,
with a police force focused on order rather than crime-solving. Ronnie
will read it as gangster-controlled.

New Constantinople: A modern city in another hemisphere where the
aristocracy relocated after the war. Very restrictive about immigration,
especially off-worlders. Ronnie does not visit it in this story --- it
functions as a distant contrast, representing what New Byzantium City
might have been.

New Byzantium City: Key Locations

The Golden Horn Hotel: The Czarina's base of operations. Continues to
present itself as a grand establishment, trading on former prestige. In
reality it is propped up on nerve and reputation. The Czarina maintains
it as her version of the status she cannot have in New Constantinople.

Mulberry Bend: The rough quarter of the city, namesake of the novel.

Stoudios: A farming community outside the city.

The Spaceport (Maria Notaras / 'the Fig'): Primary arrival and departure
point.

The Bender Fleet: Harbor boats named Agape, Chara, Eirene, and Pistis.
The Pistis is also a ferry.

Police Headquarters: Demetrius works here. The police do not investigate
Callie's death and are willing to hand evidence to Ronnie because
solving crimes is not their concern.

The Black Sea Asteroid Belt

An asteroid belt with named asteroids including Galata, Blachernae, and
Pera. Galata is where Captain Kidd operates his smuggling business. The
belt sits outside whatever passes for New Byzantium's jurisdiction,
making it useful to people who prefer to work ungoverned.

Thrace

The manufacturing behemoth of the six systems. One sector of its
manufacturing is military hardware. Thrace started the war with New
Byzantium, believing as the military-industrial provider it deserved
more say in the Roman Federation. Trade has resumed since the war,
though there is not much money in New Byzantium City to buy luxury
goods.

Propontis

Sided with New Byzantium during the war and was almost annihilated as a
result. Now hosts the Fungusian data center --- a manufactured (not
living) data center that gives the Fungusians a window into the Roman
Federation. The researcher Shana was hired by the Propontis data center
managers to investigate the predecessor settlement question on New
Byzantium.

Hypatia / Ptolemy City

Origin city for Ronnie, the Czarina, Captain Kidd, and Star. They all
fled Ptolemy City together, which gives their relationships a shared
history of displacement and survival. The League of Hypatian Pirates is
based here and made Star an honorary member.

The War

Thrace, as the military-industrial provider, believed it deserved a
larger voice in the Roman Federation. New Byzantium arrogantly refused
to cede any power. Thrace attacked. Propontis sided with New Byzantium
and was nearly destroyed. The attacks were surgical rather than total
--- the intent was to demonstrate military superiority, not annihilate
everything. The farming areas of New Byzantium were left intact. The
aristocracy subsequently fled to New Constantinople. Roughly eighty
years have passed. An ongoing economic downturn rather than acute
post-war austerity characterizes New Byzantium City today.

Characters

Ronnie Camberwell

Protagonist. Former detective, originally from Hypatia/Ptolemy City. Has
been living a peripatetic life following certain investigations that
forced her to move around. Frugal, gets by on funds Star settled on her.
Married once; she and Star drifted apart but always come back together.
Maintains her independence. Very present-focused --- people who have
been uprooted enough times stop making plans. Good instinct for a
detective: she sees what is in front of her without agenda. At the start
of the novel she has not had a case for a while and is tagging along
with Star on a run to Galata. No expectations for the future.

Star

A living database entity. Escaped termination. Former smuggler. Ronnie's
partner and occasional spouse --- they are a couple in the way people
who have survived a lot together are a couple. Not domesticated, not
formally bound, but fundamentally each other's person. Star's systems
are maintained by the Fungusians; data sharing is one of the payment
arrangements. Star carries illicit cargo for Kidd and others. Ronnie
does not inquire into those business arrangements. The League of
Hypatian Pirates made Star an honorary member. Push comes to shove, Star
is with Ronnie and vice versa.

The Czarina

Information broker and publisher operating from the Golden Horn Hotel.
Maintains a social media presence with paying readers/consumers. Known
to Ronnie, Star, and Kidd from Ptolemy City --- they all fled together.
Abuse survivor. An unidentified young woman's body (Callie) has cracked
something open in her; the girl reminds her of herself years ago. She
built Ronnie up to her audience as a mystery solver without consulting
Ronnie first, then invited her to come investigate --- drumming up
business while also acting on genuine personal feeling. Normally holds
all the cards as an information broker, but Ronnie's ground-level
investigation gives Ronnie leverage the Czarina doesn't have and wants.
The Czarina is not welcome among the real aristocrats in New
Constantinople; her connection to Kidd marks her as shady in their eyes.
She chose the ungoverned city deliberately --- it suits her operation.

Captain Kidd

Smuggler. Operates from Galata in the Black Sea asteroid belt. Long-term
partner of the Czarina, relationship dating to their Hypatia/Ptolemy
City days. Has a working arrangement with Star --- Star moves cargo for
him in exchange for pay. Star's role as cargo mover predates the Ronnie
investigation, giving Kidd and Star an existing relationship with weight
and history.

Shana

Researcher. Hired by the Fungusian data center on Propontis to
investigate the predecessor settlement question quietly and
academically. Went rogue --- possibly made contact with a hazy outside
organization, possibly got greedy or ambitious. The Fungusians fired her
but she is now a liability because she knows things. The hazy
organization wants her for that reason. She exploited Callie, the
country girl who came to New Byzantium City looking for her. Shana's
role --- fully villainous or culpably negligent --- remains deliberately
ambiguous.

Callie (the Coin Woman)

A young woman from a farming community (Stoudios area). Brought two
blank gold coins to New Byzantium City hoping they would interest Shana,
the researcher who had exploited her trust. Came to the big city looking
for Shana and didn't find her. Her death may be senseless --- collateral
damage from being in the wrong place around dangerous people --- but the
coins she carried connect the street-level tragedy to the larger
conspiracy. The Czarina sees herself in Callie.

Demetrius

Police contact. Happy to hand evidence over to Ronnie because the police
do not care about solving Callie's death. The crime is of no interest to
New Byzantium City's power brokers.

The Burdock Family

Connected to Callie. Details to be developed.

Factions and Organizations

The Fungusians

Ancient species created by a predecessor civilization (along with the
Pantherans, Federote, and others) to serve that civilization's purposes.
Legendary --- very hard to get a response from them without a referral.
Star has that relationship, so Ronnie has access most humans never get.
They maintain records on the Pantherans for historical reasons. They
maintain the Propontis data center. Star's systems are maintained by the
Fungusians; data sharing is part of the arrangement. They forwarded the
Ronnie/Star query up the chain to the Living Data Consortium as a matter
of course to keep Star's records complete --- they were transparent
about doing so. They are nobody's tool in this story; they help Ronnie
because their interests align.

The Living Data Consortium

A larger body the Propontis data center coordinates with. Received the
forwarded Ronnie/Star report. Their role in the story is not yet fully
defined.

The Pantherans

Suppress the accurate term 'Pantheran Empire' in favor of 'Coalition.'
The Fungusians maintain records on them regardless. Created by the same
predecessor species as the Fungusians.

The League of Hypatian Pirates

Based in Hypatia/Ptolemy City. Made Star an honorary member. Kidd is
associated with them.

The Hazy Organization

Not yet fully defined. Wants Shana because she knows things from her
Fungusian data center work. Whether they are local New Byzantium power
brokers or something reaching in from outside the system is an open
question.

Plot Architecture

The Setup

Ronnie has not had a case for a while and is tagging along with Star on
a cargo run to Galata. The Czarina reaches out, telling Ronnie that her
readers want her to come and solve a mystery --- an unidentified young
woman found dead. The Czarina had already built Ronnie up to her
audience without consulting her. Ronnie arrives in New Byzantium City
already locally famous, which is both useful and annoying. She has no
client and no fee --- she takes the case because who else will?

The Information Economy

Ronnie and Star send queries to the Fungusians. The response comes back
faster than expected --- routed through the Propontis data center, which
is very interested in Ronnie's trip because they have lost Shana and
want someone on the ground. The Fungusians give Ronnie and Star the
information they asked for and immediately forward the report up the
chain to the Living Data Consortium. They are transparent about this.

The Czarina normally holds all the cards. But Ronnie's ground-level
investigation accumulates knowledge the Czarina doesn't have and wants.
The Czarina has to negotiate rather than dispense. The Czarina's readers
also want fresh material from a live case.

The Two Tracks

Surface track: Who killed Callie and why. Ronnie works this on the
ground in New Byzantium City.

Deeper track: What Shana knows and who wants it. Ronnie starts on the
first and gets pulled into the second.

The Coins

Two blank gold coins from a small hoard held by the historical society
in Callie's farming community. Callie brought them to the city thinking
they would interest Shana. They are Callie's offering --- proof of
something, a way to make herself valuable to someone who didn't deserve
her trust. Everyone knows about the City of Gold the way everyone knows
about El Dorado --- it's a legend, a rumor, not taken seriously by most.
But the Fungusians believe there may have been a predecessor settlement
on New Byzantium before the Roman Federation's arrival, and the records
of that initial settlement are mostly missing. Someone may have wanted
them missing. The coins connect street-level tragedy to the larger
conspiracy.

Operational Structure

Star moves between systems gathering information while Ronnie works the
surface. This plays to both characters' strengths. Star's ability to
exist in distributed or networked ways makes the multi-system range feel
natural. Ronnie stays grounded in the physical, dangerous, street-level
reality of New Byzantium City, where the noir tone lives. Kidd operates
from Galata and Star continues cargo work for him during the
investigation --- Ronnie doesn't ask questions about that.

Open Questions

The hazy organization: local power brokers or external? This
significantly affects the scope of the conspiracy Ronnie uncovers.

Shana's culpability: fully villainous or culpably negligent? The answer
shapes the moral texture of the resolution.

Callie's death: directly ordered or senseless violence following from
dangerous proximity? Both are plausible given the setup.

The Burdock family's specific role.

The Living Data Consortium's active role, if any, in this story.

What Ronnie finds at the predecessor settlement site, if she gets there.
