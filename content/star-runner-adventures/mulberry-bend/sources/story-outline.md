 1. Setting
    - Roman Federation: 6 star systems outside of Coalition space.
      1. Bithynia
      2. Thrace
      3. Propontis
      4. Chalcedon
      5. Pontus
      6. Heraclius
    - New Byzantium, a planet in the Bithinia star system Primary
      setting for Ronnie's investigations.
    - Propontis, location of the Fungusian Network data
      center. Located on an uninhabited moon, it was built by
      automated machinery and operated by one of their AI agents.
    - The Black Sea, an asteroid belt in the Bithinia system. Site of
      both legitimate and smuggler shipping operations.
    - Thrace, the industrial heart of the Roman Federation. Likely no
      action takes place in this system in this novel but its products are
      ubiquitous in areas where living beings interact.

 3. The war
    - It took place approximately 80 years ago.
    - Thrace with its industrial base, got tired of New Byzantium's
      insistance on its primacy in the cluster.
    - Propontis sided with New Byzantium and without any protections,
      suffered catastrophic loss. Its leadership fled to Heraclius.
    - Chalcedon and Pontus remained neutral. Chalcedon has been
      quietly investing in Propontis and providing the minimum of
      humanitarian relief to its population, those who were unable to flee.
    - For the moment, Heraclius is a black box but it may have
      intentions on cluster leadership.
    - New Byzantium City had been the home of aristocratic government,
      but royalty and hangerons have relocated to New Constinopal on a
      different continent. Immigration is greatly restricted.
 4. The Czarina
    - We last saw the Czarina in ----. Although not documented, I
      think she and Kidd have moved several times since then. Although
      no longer captaining his own spacecraft, Kidd continues to
      operate in black markets.
    - The Czarina has continued to be an information broker wherever
      she lands and uses social media to drum up interest in
      controversial areas. She makes a marginal living while
      projecting her image of faded royalty. In reality, she is a
      former low-level mobster's mall who was finally able to
      breakaway from an abusive relationship and establish her
      brand. Ronnie and Star know her and Kidd from Ptolemy City where
      we first meet Ronnie when she was a police detective.
    - The Czarina was not able to establish herself in New Constinopal
      because of immigration restrictions so she has set up in the
      Golden Horn Hotel, a thread-bare image of its former
      presteige. She now deals with the local gangs, entrenpenours and
      reformers, eeking out enough to keep her brand and living
      expenses going.
    - Right now she has hit upon promoting Ronnie's detective skill to
      solve the murder of an unknown woman in Five Points on Mulberry Bend, a
      crime-ridden neighborhood down by the docks. It is generally
      known that the organized crime uses the port of Deuteronto import
      black market items.
    - The Czarina knew Ronnie and Star were coming to the Black Sea
      because Kidd has contracted with Star for a delivery lucrative
      enough to get Star to come.
    - Ronnie came along because her investigations had dried up at
      their previous base.
    - The Czarina eeds to be kept in the loop of Ronnie's
      investigations by being able to supply Ronnie with more clues
      and background information.
