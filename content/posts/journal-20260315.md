Getting back into my 250 Sweet Friday goal for writing every day. I have
been greatly distracted this past week by re-imagining my /Notes to
Myself/ writing site. Maybe its title is not yet configured for this?
Anyway, there has been a lot of work and exploring to set the site up
with a blog link and room for adding other writing links in the future.

Along with this project, I have been surfacing older blog posts and
starting to go through them. I found
[[https:/*rillonline.github.io*blog/my-blog-in-2021.html][this one]]
that explains exactly what I am doing and feeling right now. The post is
dated August, 2021.

I also found
[[https:/*https:**rillonline.github.io*blog/holy-name.html][HolyName]].
It's dated January 1, 2020. It's the only blog post for the year. They
start up again in 2021 as far as I can tell. Stay tuned.

What makes the
[[https:/*rillonline.github.io*blog/holy-name.html][Holy Name]] post
interesting is my reflection on the mind of Christ. I still agree with
what I wrote there.

How free do I feel to express my faith in public? I've loosened up a bit
about this in the past six years. Six years! Amazing.

We've been through a lot in the past six years. Christian nationalism
coming to the forefront and the aggressive stance of some evangelicals
has changed my thinking.

Last year I said to some people:

- I want the cross back.
- I want the flag back.

These symbols are as much mine as they are theirs.

If faith matters at all, and I certainly think it does, I pray every day
for peace, justice, truth, for the needy not to be forgotten or the hope
of the poor not to be taken away. I pray to trust in God and that my
trust not be in vain.

It could certainly be pointed out to me that these prayers don't seem to
be answered. We are in a costly and ill-conceived war. People are going
hungry and are without sufficient health care. Energy prices are up.
Food prices are up.

There is no way to have a control group on this. I have decided that
these prayers are effective. Things could be a whole lot worse. Things
might even get a whole lot worse, but prayer is what I am encouraged to
do so I do it. Part of my implicit position is that by praying I become
more confident in the salutary nature of prayer.
