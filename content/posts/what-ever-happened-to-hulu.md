Our friend Hulu went off to the Paws Forward program in prison. She
barked for 5 minutes straight when she first arrived. We were hopeful
she would settle in.

Wrong!

The prisoners found Hulu to be smart and willing to be trained, but
she did bark--a definite no-no--and she pulled. They described her
behavior as aggressive. I might characterize it as enthusiastic. But
she's a strong dog and almost pulled an inmate off his feet.

So she got kicked out of the program. Imagine, failing prison.

It is not the norm. The puppies are expected to go through the prison
program.

She was evaluated last week by a guide dog mobility instructor
(GDMI). He was my instructor and trained Nina. Hulu floored him. She
started barking at a statue and didn't stop. She barked at people and
was basically out of control for a prospective guide dog who needs to
be quiet and behave with decorum. So she failed.

It looks like she already has a new career all lined up. She is going
to a couple who needs a new pet. I am certain Hulu will delight
them. She was a fun dog. We already know that she plays well with
their grandchildren.
