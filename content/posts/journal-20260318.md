I spent two days interacting with [[https:/*claude.ai*][Claude]]
concerning two story fragments that were similar enough that I thought
they might be combined but I wasn't sure how. You can read about it
[[https:/*rillonline.github.io*mulberry-bend/index.html][here]]. The
interaction was a great way to brainstorm and have my ideas reflected
back to me.

The general outline of the story is done. I'm basically a pantser so
knowing where I am going is different. I will let the story take me
where it takes me.

Right now a character we just called *the researcher* has caught my
attention. I've been writing a scene with her which chronologically
comes near the end of the book but I think it's important to write this
chapter. It is one perspective on the dead woman who connected these two
fragments. It is a perspective Ronnie, one of my main recurring
characters, doesn't share. She never knew the dead woman but Ronnie
still has opinions about her and about the researcher as well.

[[https:/*claude.ai*][Claude]] called this piece “Where to go from here”
or words to that effect. I think one of the ways to go is to continue
writing this scene. It will shape what Tamara, the researcher's boss,
thinks about her and that is another scene I can write, again at the end
of the narrative.

Maybe if I write the end I will stay focused on getting there! I've
written books chronologically, but never with scenes that were not
connected.
