I have been doing a lot of work with Emacs and moving my published
writing from github to my local network on my pi4b. As a result, I
haven't been working much on my novel. So I thought I would start
thinking aloud via these journal entries since they are now literally
notes to myself.

I've never been much for world building. My Star Runner Universe has
evolved. The world continues to make sense more or less. I wandered a
bit in the region of space controlled by the old races and I may
revisit that at some point, but I think this new group of star systems
has promise.

I've imagined New Byzantium City as a city which still shows the
destruction brought about by a war. This poses questions.

- When did the war happen?
- Why was there a war?
  - Who was involved?
- What caused it?

These are basic journalistic questions. Let's see if I can
make a stab at answering them today.

** When Did the War Happen?

We talk a lot lately about the post World War II era. That is
roughly eighty years ago. Is eighty years enough time? Too
long? Not enough? Ronnie will clearly see the lack of repairs,
but also the ongoing poor economy but the city's refusal to
cave. The Golden Horn Hotel continues to pretend to be a grand
place and to remind everyone of its former prestige. The
inhabitants are not in a post war austerity regime but rather
an ongoing economic downturn. Trade has resumed with the
Thracians though there is not much money in evidence to buy
luxury goods. New Byzantium itself is the bread basket of the
six star systems as it has the best climate for growing human
food. It can't, however, use this as a strangle hold on the
other star systems. (I don't know exactly why. Perhaps it's
just scale and holding the star economy together.) That would
actually make sense. All these systems are quite connected,
intentionally so. That was the master plan, at least this is
what the Fungusians hypothesize.

** What was the War About

Thrace is the manufacturing behemoth. One sector of its manufacturing
is military hardware. The Six Systems (I definitely need a better
word. Roman Empire doesn't sound right.) As the provider, it thought
it should have a bigger say in the Roman Federation. (This title will
work for now.) New Byzantium arrogantly refused to cede any power and
the Thracians attacked. I'm still hazy about the other systems and
their place except for Propontis which sided with New Byzantium and
was almost annihilated.

*** The Fungusian Data Center

Propontis will be the place where the
Fungusian data center is located. This is a manufactured data center,
not a live one. It allows the Fungusians a look into the Roman
Federation and the researcher Shana is hired by the managers to go to
New Byzantium.

** Aftermath on new Byzantium

The aristocracy fled New Byzantium City for another hemisphere. There
is a modern city there. This begs the question why the Czarina isn't
there but is in New Byzantium. It may be as simple as New
Constantinople being very restrictive concerning immigration and
especially offworlders.

** Who Runs New Byzantium

With the aristocracy gone those that couldn't make the move were
left. The city struggled for some time--the Urban Wars as J. D. Robb
calls them in the Eve Dallas series. Eventually, the power brokers got
basic services up and running and the city persists. Taxes are low to
encourage people to stay. The farming areas were still in tact since
the attacks were done with pinpoint accuracy. The intent was not to
totally destroy everything but to demonstrate military superiority. So
long as the New Byzantium aristocracy doesn't overplay their hand,
they will be left alone. New Byzantium also prospers with a seafood
industry. They are near some very lucrative fishing grounds,
supplying off-worlders with delicacies. As with much of New Byzantium
City Ronnie will think of this as gangster controlled. new Byzantium
City is a violent and dangerous place.

There is a police force but its main goal is to keep order rather than
to solve crimes. That is why, when she goes to police headquarters she
finds no one is investigating Callie's death and Demetrius is happy to
turn evidence over to her. He doesn't care one way or another if she
solves the crime. And, in the end, the crime is of no interest to the
power brokers of New Byzantium.
